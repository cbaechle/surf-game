# Art credits

All scenery, the surfboard, stars, clouds, land, particles and the placeholder surfer are
drawn procedurally in code (`src/render/textures.ts`, `src/render/art.ts`) and shaders
(`src/render/sky.ts`, `src/render/water.ts`); they are original to this project.

## Fonts

| File | What | Source | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fonts/fredoka-700-latin.woff2` | Fredoka, Bold (700), Latin subset, as served by Google Fonts | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fredoka (repository: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/fredoka) | Milena Brandão, Hafontia (The Fredoka Project Authors) | SIL Open Font License 1.1, full text in `fonts/Fredoka-OFL.txt` |

The OFL allows the font to be bundled and redistributed with software, including
commercially, as long as the licence travels with it and the font is not sold on its own.
Licence checked against `METADATA.pb` and `OFL.txt` in the google/fonts repository.
